## Objective quality

* Candidate pooling (default pool vs the full player universe, horizon 5): objective lost p50 0.000, max 0.000 points; identical optimum in 100% of states.
* MILP vs the greedy one-transfer heuristic (horizon 5, same objective): MILP higher by p50 5.53, mean 22.21, max 97.25 objective points; strictly better in 94% of states (equal where at most one transfer is optimal).
* Opening all usable chips (horizon 5): objective gain p50 3.10, max 51.95 (warm-started from the no-chip optimum, so never negative by construction).
* Forced 1-second limit on 8-GW chip-open problems without a warm start (harsher than production, which warm-starts chip problems from the no-chip optimum): 48 of 48 hit the limit; 26 found no feasible plan in time; 22 of the 22 returned incumbents were valid after polishing.

## Determinism and stability

* Re-solving identical inputs reproduced the same first-week action and objective in 100% of states (HiGHS single thread, fixed seed).
* With every player's forecast multiplied by lognormal noise (σ = 0.05), the first-week action was unchanged in 38% of solves — a measure of how close competing moves are, not an error.
* Mean first-week transfers at horizon 5 by squad type: active 1.5, optimal 0.0, random 8.2, stale 4.8.
