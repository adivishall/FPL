## 1. Intended use

Inputs to a decision engine for Fantasy Premier League squad management: per-player, per-gameweek
**distributions** of FPL points (not point estimates), start/minutes probabilities and price-move
probabilities. Outputs are used inside an optimiser and paired Monte Carlo comparisons and are shown
to the user with their uncertainty. They are *not* guarantees of points, rank or price movement and
must not be presented as such (§66, §65).

Out of scope: betting, any use on fixtures/leagues other than the Premier League FPL game, and
players with no history in the training data beyond their position/price prior (they receive the
prior, flagged by zero exposure in the rate posterior).

## 2. Model structure (ADR-0006)

```
team strength (Poisson attack/defence MAP, xG-blended, Laplace posterior)
        │  μ_home, μ_away per fixture
        ▼
joint fixture simulation (common random numbers per fixture × player)
  goals ~ Poisson(μ) → scorer/assister chosen among players *on the pitch at goal time*
  in proportion to shrunk goal/assist shares; minutes from the minutes model; saves, defensive
  actions (NB), cards, penalties, own goals from shrunk per-90 rates; BPS from a learned linear
  map + player offset → bonus via official tie rules; points via the versioned ruleset
        ▼
per player × GW samples → mean, quantiles, P(≥2/6/10/15), component means, minutes quantiles
```

* **Minutes**: LightGBM P(start) trained on multi-horizon rows (horizon is a feature, so return
  probabilities are modelled), isotonic-calibrated on the chronologically latest 15 % of the
  training window; P(sub | no start); multiclass minutes-if-start buckets; empirical sub buckets.
  Live FPL status / chance of playing is applied by a separate, versioned **availability layer**
  (uncalibrated configuration — no historical news exists to learn from; ADR-0001 #7).
* **Rates**: Bühlmann–Straub credibility shrinkage `(x + κm)/(E + κ)` with κ = φ/(c·m) estimated
  by a split-half design (noise scale φ and between-player CV² c). Out-of-sample check: the fitted
  κ is the minimum of next-10-GW error for goal share, assist share, DC and yellow cards and within
  0.8 % of it for saves; raw rates are 47 %–3,400 % worse, position/price priors 5 %–86 % worse.
* **BPS**: weighted ridge regression of BPS on match events per position (learned, not hard-coded,
  so 2026-27 rule changes enter with data; ADR-0001 #13).
* **Team strength**: rolling-origin selected hyper-parameters (a season only uses settings chosen
  on earlier seasons). +0.12 / +0.055 nats per match over the league average (2024-25 / 2025-26).

## 4. Evaluation (114 decision cutoffs, 2023-24 → 2025-26, horizon 5, 405,942 player × GW rows)

Regenerated on 2026-10-04 under schedule rule S1 (ADR-0004: the fixture schedule as published at
each cutoff). The earlier numbers (396,696 rows, RMSE 1.927) read the archive's final schedule;
the conclusions below did not change.

**Point accuracy, horizon 0** (RMSE primary; paired bootstrap over cutoffs, 95 % CI):

| Forecaster | RMSE all | RMSE regulars | Spearman all |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decomposed MC (mean)** | **1.924** | **2.955** | **0.706** |
| Direct LightGBM | 1.934 | 2.968 | 0.700 |
| ppg × availability | 2.031 | 3.125 | 0.671 |
| Recent form | 2.035 | 3.138 | 0.673 |
| FPL-style heuristic (approximation) | 2.053 | 3.157 | 0.682 |
| Position mean | 2.351 | 3.520 | 0.083 |

MC − direct LightGBM: −0.0084 RMSE [−0.0104, −0.0063] (all), −0.0142 [−0.0180, −0.0104]
(regulars). MC − best simple baseline: −0.093 [−0.111, −0.078]. The advantage holds at every
horizon (h4: 2.060 vs 2.070 direct, 2.151 best baseline).

**Distributional quality, horizon 0**: CRPS 0.628 vs 0.722 for a position × role climatology
(−0.068 [−0.071, −0.065]); P(≥6) Brier 0.055 / ECE 0.003; randomised-PIT central 80 % coverage
0.804 and 50 % coverage 0.505; PIT histogram max deviation 0.006 → well calibrated. The inclusive
[p10, p90] interval covers 0.938 because points are integers (40 % of rows have p10 = p90); this is
why the first coverage gate was mis-specified (§6).

**Minutes, horizon 0**: P(start) Brier 0.083 vs 0.101 (rate baseline), log loss 0.268 vs 0.355,
ECE 0.011 vs 0.021; expected-minutes MAE 14.1 vs 16.0.

**Price change** (76,516 predictions): rise log loss 0.059 vs 0.084 trend baseline vs 0.102 base
rate (AUC 0.957 / 0.792 / 0.478); fall log loss 0.151 vs 0.176 vs 0.196 (AUC 0.863 / 0.733);
ECE 0.0012 (rise), 0.0047 (fall).

**P1 research — ensemble and conformal intervals** (`ml/reports/ensemble_conformal.md`):
* A weighted ensemble of the MC mean and the direct LightGBM, with the weight chosen on earlier
  seasons only (0.75 / 0.65 on MC), improves horizon-0 RMSE by 0.0044 (2024-25) and 0.0005
  (2025-26). **Not adopted**: the decision engine needs one coherent joint distribution (lineups,
  captaincy and paired plan comparisons use the samples), and a mean-only blend would make the
  displayed mean inconsistent with the distribution for a ≤ 0.2 % gain.
* Conformal intervals with temporal care (calibration on the previous 8 cutoffs' rows whose
  outcomes were known): residual conformal reaches its 0.80 target (0.805) but is wider than the
  MC interval (3.19 vs 2.70 points at h0) and symmetric; CQR adjustment of the MC quantiles is
  q = 0 at every horizon because the MC intervals already over-cover integer outcomes.
  **Not adopted** for display; the PIT-based coverage monitor (§6) is the calibration control.

Reliability diagrams: `ml/reports/figures/forecast_reliability_6plus.svg`,
`forecast_reliability_start.svg`, `forecast_pit.svg`, `price_reliability.svg`.

## 5. Feature importance and stability (§57.2)

`ml/reports/feature_importance.md`: rank stability across six retrains 0.968 (start), 0.974
(direct points), 0.975 (price rise). The only flagged feature, `start_rate_short` (CV 0.69), was
investigated: it is NaN in seasons whose source lacks the `starts` column, so its share rises as
more training rows carry it (0.005 → 0.024); the combined share of the collinear minutes features
(`mins_ewm_short`, `mins_last1`, `app_rate_short`, `start_rate_short`) is stable (CV 0.077).

## 6. Governance (§71, ADR-0008)

* Promotion gates are declared in `config/models/*.yaml` and evaluated by
  `fpl_forecasting.governance.evaluate_gates`; results are stored with the version in the registry
  (`fpl_storage.registry`: content-addressed artifacts, candidate → approved → production, one
  production version per model, immediate rollback to the previously promoted version).
* **Gate outcomes on the evaluation above**: minutes@1.0.0 PASSED; price_change@1.0.0 PASSED;
  points@1.0.0 **FAILED** (`interval_80_coverage` = 0.938, target 0.75–0.90). Diagnosis: the gate,
  not the model, was mis-specified for integer outcomes (see §4). points@1.1.0 replaces it with the
  PIT-based central coverage (0.803; 0.804 after the S1 re-evaluation; target 0.77–0.83) → PASSED. The correction was made after
  seeing the result and is recorded as such in the config header and the report's gate history.
* Drift: PSI per feature (training reference vs current snapshot), residual RMSE ratio, bias and
  ECE; retraining triggers = schedule (4 GWs) **or** degradation (RMSE ratio > 1.15, |bias| > 0.4,
  ECE > 0.05) **or** > 3 features with PSI > 0.25 (`config/models/points.yaml: retraining`).
* Every prediction run stores feature snapshot id, data snapshot id, model versions with config
  refs, ruleset version, simulation seed and sample count (`prediction_run_id`).

## 7. Limitations and risks

* **No historical availability news**: injuries/suspensions announced before a deadline are not in
  the archive, so historical evaluation measures the model *without* that signal. Live use applies
  the availability layer, whose mapping is an uncalibrated assumption.
* **Price model information set**: the archive is gameweek-granular, so the strongest real-world
  signal (net transfers in the final days before a deadline) is unobservable historically.
* **Defensive contributions (2025-26 rule)**: one season of data; rates shrink strongly toward
  position priors.
* **Goalkeeper goals / rare events**: never observed in the data (BPS goal coefficient for GK is 0);
  scoring itself is rule-exact.
* **Promoted teams**: priors only until matches accrue.
* **Correlated outcomes beyond a fixture** (e.g. weather, referee) are not modelled; within-fixture
  correlation (goals → assists → clean sheets → bonus) is.
* **2026-27**: only one live snapshot is available in this environment (FPL API host blocked);
  live-mode quality cannot be measured here.
